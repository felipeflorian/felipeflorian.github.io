Andrés Felipe Florián Quitián

Ph.D. student in Engineering at Universidad de los Andes, working on post-quantum cryptography: code-based schemes, cryptanalysis, and decoding attacks that exploit posterior or side-channel leakage.

Research

My work studies the hardness of syndrome decoding, the problem underlying code-based cryptosystems such as Classic McEliece. In particular, I look at how partial information about the secret error vector, whether probabilistic hints or leakage from an implementation, changes the cost of recovering it.

My master's thesis compared how Reed–Muller codes and random codes behave under this kind of posterior leakage, combining experiments with a theoretical analysis. I continue this line in my Ph.D., supervised by Prof. Valérie Gauthier-Umaña and Prof. Ricardo Villanueva-Polanco.
